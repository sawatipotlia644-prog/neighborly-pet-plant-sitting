Trust and safety

Helpful, respectful, and easy to coordinate.

✓

Communicate clearly

Share timing, expectations, and any care instructions before agreeing to a task.

✓

Leave good instructions

Write down feeding schedules, watering needs, and any important home access details.

✓

Confirm dates and responsibilities

Check the exact dates, visit times, and what each helper is expected to do before starting.

✓

Respect home and privacy

Only enter spaces as agreed, keep personal information private, and respect boundaries.
